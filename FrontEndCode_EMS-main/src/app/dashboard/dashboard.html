Executive Dashboard Guide

Every metric on this console is computed live from the enterprise database:

  • Total Workforce — Active registered users and employee accounts.
  • Project Portfolio — Evaluated across milestones, completion rates, and overdue ticket counts.
  • Delivery Velocity — Percentage of total sprint tickets resolved and closed.
  • Billable Hours — Total work hours logged across daily task-wise timesheets.
